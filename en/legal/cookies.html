What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your computer when you visit a website. Among other things, they remember your preferences, such as your chosen language. On a later visit, this cookie is sent back to the website, which then recognises your browser.

Cookies generally have an expiry date. "Session cookies" are deleted when you close your browser; "persistent cookies" remain stored on your device for longer.
