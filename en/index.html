Frequently asked questions

What property owners ask us before outsourcing.

Who represents me at co-owners' general meetings?

We do. EXYRO represents you at co-owners' general meetings and makes your voice heard, so you don't have to attend. See what we handle for you.

Is everything automated? Will I speak to a real person?

Yes, you will speak to a real person, and we are committed to that. We use digital tools and AI for repetitive tasks, so we can spend more time on people. A team of 11 is at your service and we have no intention of making it smaller. See our team.

What happens if a tenant stops paying?

You don't face the procedure alone: we send reminders, issue formal notice and, if necessary, refer the matter to the justice of the peace on your behalf. See what you gain from it.

Do I need a new lease if I already have a tenant in place?

No. We take over the management of your existing lease, with nothing changing for your current tenant.

Does EXYRO operate throughout Belgium?

Yes, we cover the entire Belgian territory. See our numbers.

How do I check my income and documents?

Through your online owner portal, available around the clock. See how it works.

I have a question specific to my situation, who answers?

Ask it directly to the EXYRO property assistant, or write to your future dedicated contact.

More questions about property?

Our assistant answers your general questions about renting and investing in Belgium: it explains, warns you, and shows where to check the information (IPI, FPS Finance, notaries, Regions). For your personal situation, a member of the team takes over.

Virtual assistant: its answers are informative and do not replace legal advice or a notary's opinion.
