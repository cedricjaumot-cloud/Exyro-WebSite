Frequently asked questions

What property owners ask us before outsourcing.

What happens if a tenant stops paying?

You don't face the procedure alone: we send reminders, issue formal notice and, if necessary, refer the matter to the justice of the peace on your behalf. See what you gain from it.

Do I need a new lease if I already have a tenant in place?

No. We take over the management of your existing lease, with nothing changing for your current tenant.

Does EXYRO operate throughout Belgium?

Yes, we cover the entire Belgian territory. See our numbers.

How do I check my income and documents?
